How this works

Each body has 10 motorised joints (both legs and both arms are separate) and a hand that can close. Every 1/60 s a small neural network reads what the body senses (balance, joint angles, feet, the ground ahead, where the glass is) and sets a target for every motor.

Nobody programs the movements. Training tries 48 slightly different brains, keeps the best and repeats. Difficulty grows as they improve: shoves, then sticks and logs on the ground, and the grip assist fades away.

Teach lets you move the limbs yourself while the brain keeps fighting you. Your keyframes become a demonstration that training can learn from, only if you choose it.

New agents start knowing nothing. Kit, Juno and Pax are trained examples.
