How this works

Each agent's body has 10 motorised joints and a hand that can close. Every 1/60 s a small neural network reads what the body senses (balance, joint angles, feet on the ground, where the glass is) and sets a target angle and strength for every motor.

Nobody programs the movements. Training tries 48 slightly different brains, keeps the ones that score best, and repeats. Two agents trained on the same skill end up moving differently, because each has its own random seed.

Skills build on each other: Walk and Pick up the glass start from the agent's Stand brain, because you have to balance before you can do anything else.
