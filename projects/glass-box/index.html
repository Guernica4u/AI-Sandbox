        Goal: build a house on the plot by the end of day 20. It takes days: they work from 06:00 to 20:00 and sleep at night (1× speed = 1 game minute per second; nights fly by). First they lay out a ring of logs by hand, which decides the floor plan; then they raise the walls 6 logs high and put a roof on. Nobody tells them how to make tools: they experiment with sticks, stones and bark until something works. Whoever works it out can teach the others (or get asked), and anyone standing close by can pick it up just by watching. What they teach is what they believe, so it can be wrong. An axe turns trees into logs, and every log is placed by hand: straight on, round a corner, or leaving a doorway. Tight joints keep the warmth in and a bigger floor is nicer. Nobody tells them this; they learn it from how their houses are judged.
